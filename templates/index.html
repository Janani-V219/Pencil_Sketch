Behind The Scenes

How The OpenCV Vision Pipeline Works

Real-time image synthesis without heavy neural network latency

01

Grayscale Extraction

Converts RGB/BGR color channels into luminance intensities using standard ITU-R 601-2 luma weights, stripping distracting chromatic data.

cv2.cvtColor(img, cv2.COLOR_BGR2GRAY)
02

Inversion & Gaussian Blur

Calculates bitwise negative and applies a dynamically sized 2D Gaussian kernel to isolate edge high-frequency variations from low-frequency gradients.

cv2.GaussianBlur(255 - gray, (k, k), 0)
03

Color-Dodge Division

Applies mathematical color dodge division: dividing the original grayscale by the inverted blurred mask to produce pencil hatching and strokes.

cv2.divide(gray, 255 - blur, scale=256)
04

Graphite Tone Mapping

Applies gamma non-linear curves, synthetic paper grain micro-textures, and adaptive CLAHE contrast curves tailored for each artistic style.

cv2.LUT(sketch, gamma_table) + grain
